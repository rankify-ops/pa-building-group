How It Works

Four steps, no mystery

From the first phone call to the day you get your house back.

1

Tell us the idea

Call or send the form. A rough sketch, a Pinterest board, or just a feeling that the kitchen is too small — all fine.

2

We come and look

A proper site visit, measurements, and an itemised written quote. No charge, no obligation, no sales pitch.

3

We build it

Programme locked in, trades booked, permits managed. You get regular updates and a number that gets answered.

4

Handover & after

Final walk-through, defect list closed out, warranties handed over — and we still pick up the phone next year.
