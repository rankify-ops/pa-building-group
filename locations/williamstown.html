Building into Williamstown’s housing stock

Williamstown is characterised by coastal heritage homes where salt air and overlays both matter. Houses like these are why we do most of our work at the rear — the street frontage is worth keeping, and everything behind it usually needs rethinking.

We take on rear extensions, whole-home renovations, kitchens and bathrooms, and second dwellings across Williamstown. Where a heritage or neighbourhood character overlay applies, we handle the planning application, the heritage advice and the council liaison rather than handing it back to you.

Every job starts the same way: we come and look at the house, we tell you honestly what’s realistic for your budget, and we put an itemised quote in writing. If we think you’d be better off spending less, we’ll say so.

  • Rear and second-storey extensions
  • Whole-home and room-by-room renovations
  • Kitchens, bathrooms, ensuites and laundries
  • Heritage overlay work and period restoration
  • Granny flats, studios and home offices
  • Commercial fit-outs and lease make-goods
  • Ongoing maintenance, carpentry and repairs
