Our Story

Forty years in Melbourne housing

Trading as PA Building and Maintenance Services P/L, PA Building Group has spent over forty years in Melbourne residential construction — home additions and renovations, kitchen and bathroom upgrades, outdoor living areas, property maintenance and pre-sale facelifts.

Four decades in the same city means very little is genuinely new to us. We’ve worked on Victorian terraces, Edwardian weatherboards, Californian bungalows, interwar brick and every era of post-war housing since. Each has its own rules and its own surprises, and we’ve learnt them the way everyone does: by opening walls.

We’re a hands-on outfit. The person who quotes your job is on site while it’s being built, and is still the person who answers the phone a year later. That’s deliberate, and it’s the main reason our work comes from referrals.

Our Values

Say the number

Answer the phone

You get a direct number for the person running your job. Not a switchboard, not a form.

Finish it

The defect list gets closed out before we ask for final payment. We come back afterwards for the small stuff.
