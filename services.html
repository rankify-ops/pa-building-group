How We Work

The same on every job, big or small

Itemised quotes

You see what each part costs. Where something genuinely can’t be priced until walls are open, we say so and put a realistic figure against it.

One team, all trades

Carpenters, plumbers, electricians, plasterers and tilers booked and sequenced by us. You manage nothing.

A programme you can plan around

Real dates, and a phone call if anything shifts. Not silence for three weeks.

Licensed, insured, certified

Permits, engineering, inspections and compliance documentation handled and handed over.

Defects closed out

We finish the list before we ask for the final payment, not after.

We come back

Small stuff after handover gets sorted. That’s the whole reason clients call us again.
